This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use Track Rush 3D on Android.

Track Rush 3D may use Google AdMob for advertising and Firebase Analytics for app analytics. When these services are enabled, they may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, approximate location derived from IP address, and diagnostic data. The information processed depends on the services integrated, their configuration, your consent choices, and applicable requirements.

Information may be used to operate and improve the game, understand usage and performance, show and measure ads, diagnose technical issues, and support in-app purchases.

Third-Party Services

Google may process information through AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Google Play Billing if these services are enabled in the game. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies:

In-App Purchases

If offered, in-app purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles payment details. The game may receive purchase information needed to validate and provide purchased items.

Sharing and Retention

Information may be processed by service providers described above, or disclosed when required by law or needed to protect the game and its users. Information is retained according to the purpose for which it was collected and the applicable service-provider settings and policies.

Children's Privacy

The game and its advertising and analytics settings should be configured for its intended audience and applicable child-privacy requirements. A parent or guardian who has a privacy concern may contact the developer using the address below.

Your Choices and Requests

You may manage available advertising and privacy choices through your device or Google account. For questions or privacy-related requests, contact the developer. Requests will be handled as required by applicable law.
